Global developmental delay vs intellectual disability

Global developmental delay and intellectual disability are not interchangeable terms. Age, stability of measures and the clinical question influence which description is appropriate.

By the Talk+ team 18 October 2026 3 min read
Loading reads…

What does global developmental delay usually mean?

The term is often used in young children when there are significant delays in more than one developmental area and intellectual and adaptive functioning cannot yet be characterised reliably. Follow-up may be needed because abilities and cooperation during testing change with age.

What is assessed for intellectual disability?

Diagnostic systems consider intellectual functioning and adaptive behaviour, with onset during development. Adaptive behaviour includes conceptual, social and practical everyday skills. An isolated IQ score does not represent the whole profile and is not enough for a conclusion.

Can other conditions occur at the same time?

Yes. Language differences, autism, medical conditions, hearing, motor and learning needs may coexist or affect results. The report should describe what was measured, testing conditions, limitations and areas needing support. Do not interpret a term without asking the professional what it means in that case.

In short

  • Global developmental delay is often an early description while a profile is being clarified.
  • Intellectual disability involves intellectual and adaptive functioning; an IQ score alone is not enough.

Conclusion

Ask the team which term was used, under which system and based on which assessed areas. A label does not replace an individual description of strengths, barriers and support.
