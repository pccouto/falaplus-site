First week with Talk+: a gentle start

The first week with Talk+ can be about discovering preferences rather than completing games or reaching scores. Notice what the child understands, chooses and wants to try again.

By the Talk+ team 6 November 2026 3 min read
Loading reads…

How can you organise a first week with Talk+?

Start by creating a profile and choosing the relevant reading option. Explore the PIN-protected Adult Area and available settings. Calm mode can reduce visual stimulation when helpful. There is no universal sensory setting: observe the individual child’s response.

The suggestions below are flexible examples, not a compulsory schedule or a screen-time recommendation. Use only one part, repeat a familiar activity or leave the app unused for several days. It should fit around rest, movement, relationships and varied play.

How can you try it in practice?

  1. At first, explore a message in Communicate and respond to the choice.
  2. At another moment, try Walk at a level available on the plan.
  3. Offer an available activity in Play.
  4. Return to a favourite option and adjust stimulation or difficulty when needed.
  5. Discuss what worked and choose one idea to continue away from the screen.

A concrete situation to try

One child may prefer repeating the same game across several occasions. Another may only want to hear an instrument or choose cards. Record a simple observation such as “liked animals” or “needed help dragging”. This is more useful for choosing the next experience than setting a target for minutes or stars.

What can we conclude?

What the app offers

  • The described features offer resources for communication, games and exploration.
  • Adults can observe comfort, interests and the help needed.

What has not been demonstrated

  • These suggestions do not demonstrate clinical efficacy of Talk+.
  • Scores, stars and time spent are not developmental measures.

Frequently asked questions

Must the app be used every day?

No. This is flexible and does not require daily use.

Is there a first-week score target?

No. Focus on the experience and preferences.

Can families start without Premium?

Yes. Explore free Communicate and other activities included in the free plan.

Can the app assess development?

No. Usage observations do not replace professional assessment.

This article is for information only and does not replace an assessment by a health professional. If you are worried about a child’s development, talk to their doctor or paediatrician.
