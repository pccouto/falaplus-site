Assessment and diagnosis · Reports and development

Should I take an app report to an appointment?

Taking a game report to an appointment may help describe a digital activity. Sharing it is optional and does not replace everyday examples.

By the Talk+ team 18 October 2026 3 min read
Loading reads…

When can taking a game report to an appointment help?

A summary may recall tasks completed and responses recorded during a set period. Explain how the data were collected and whether prompts were used. The professional can say whether they are relevant to the clinical question.

How can you prepare for the discussion?

Bring one or two clear questions and examples from home, school or other settings. Ask about a secure way to share documents and who will have access to the information.

What should you avoid when reading the record?

Do not treat more correct responses as proof of broad learning or compare results with another child. If you have a concern, seek professional guidance rather than waiting for more weeks of records.

In short

  • Sharing a digital activity record is optional and may help if it answers a clear question.
  • Explain its limits and add observations from other settings.

Conclusion

A digital activity document can be supplementary information. The discussion should focus on concerns, the child’s history and observations across settings.
