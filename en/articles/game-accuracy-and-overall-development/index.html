Assessment and diagnosis · Reports and development

Does a game score measure development?

Game accuracy rates describe responses recorded during a specific activity. They do not measure intelligence, language or overall development.

By the Talk+ team 18 October 2026 3 min read
Loading reads…

How should you interpret a game accuracy rate?

Check how many responses were included, in which task and over what period. Familiarity, prompts, tiredness and distraction can affect performance. With few attempts, the percentage may vary considerably.

Does a correct game response mean a skill is used in daily life?

Not necessarily. Selecting an answer on a screen does not show that a child uses the skill spontaneously in conversation, at school or in another routine. Generalisation should be observed in relevant settings.

What should you ask a professional?

Ask what the indicator counts, what it leaves out and whether it helps answer the specific concern. Formal assessment combines appropriate methods with history, observation and everyday functioning.

In short

  • An accuracy rate characterises responses recorded during one task.
  • It is not a standardised test or a measure of overall ability.

Conclusion

Use a percentage as limited contextual information. Understanding development calls for a broader assessment suited to the child and the question.
