Dyslexia at ages 6 and 7: early signs

Dyslexia at ages 6 and 7: early signs is a common search among families and professionals. A useful answer must recognise individual differences, explain limits and offer practical next steps without promising outcomes.

By the Talk+ team 30 October 2026 3 min read
Loading reads…

What is important to understand?

Signs may include difficulty learning letter–sound links, segmenting and blending sounds, reading new words, developing accuracy and fluency or spelling consistently.

What cautions are needed?

Errors during early literacy are common and one sign does not diagnose dyslexia. Teaching quality and duration, language, hearing, vision, language skills and opportunities matter.

What can you do next?

Speak with school about persistent difficulty and request appropriate assessment. Explicit, systematic teaching of phonemic awareness, sound–letter links, decoding and spelling can begin without waiting for a label.
