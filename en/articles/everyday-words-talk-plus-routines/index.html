Everyday words in Talk+: from games to routines

Everyday words in Talk+ appear with pictures and voice in themes connected to children’s lives. The opportunity is to link game content with real people, objects, actions and experiences.

By the Talk+ team 6 November 2026 3 min read
Loading reads…

How do you choose everyday words in Talk+?

The app includes more than 1,000 words across 25 themes. This offers variety, not a checklist every child must finish. Choose a small set connected to what is happening: food for snack time, animals for a story or actions for a game.

Pictures and voice present content within an activity. Recognising a game picture does not prove a word will be used spontaneously elsewhere. To explore that connection, return to the actual object or experience and welcome gestures, pointing and picture choices too.

How can you try it in practice?

  1. Pick a theme that connects with the day’s routine.
  2. Explore a few words in an accessible activity without requesting repetition after every picture.
  3. Return to one word during real play, reading or a familiar routine.

A concrete situation to try

After finding an apple in a game, look at a real apple: its colour, skin and what you can do with it. You might say “red apple” or “cut the apple”, depending on the situation. Snack time need not become a series of questions. Aim to open up conversation and participation.

Frequently asked questions

Must children explore all 1,000 words?

No. Choose vocabulary that is relevant and interesting now.

Does the app only contain object names?

No. Themes also include actions, feelings, people and routines.

Does a correct answer mean the word will be used elsewhere?

Not necessarily. These are different contexts.

Should adults ask children to repeat every word?

No. Listening, choosing, pointing and commenting can also be part of the experience.
