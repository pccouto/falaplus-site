Assessment and diagnosis · App guide

Weekly game report: what does it show?

Weekly game reports can organize records from a digital activity and help recall what happened in that setting. They do not observe a child in everyday life or measure overall development on their own.

By the Talk+ team 18 October 2026 3 min read
Loading reads…

What data can a weekly game report include?

Depending on the system, a report may show activity time, responses, tasks or words explored. Each indicator describes only what was defined and recorded. Check the period, collection method, unit of measurement and whether help was provided before interpreting it.

What can they not establish?

A digital record does not show that a skill has transferred to home, school or community settings; it cannot confirm or rule out a disorder. Developmental assessment considers history, observation, functioning and information from different settings.

How can you share one carefully?

Select information related to a clear question and explain the conditions in which it was collected. Do not compare percentages between children: tasks, prompts and circumstances may differ. Ask a professional whether the document adds useful information.

In short

  • A game report describes records from a limited digital activity.
  • It does not assess overall development or replace professional observation and assessment.

Conclusion

Read these documents as contextual, supplementary information. Discuss developmental concerns with qualified professionals rather than waiting for more game data.
