Assessment and diagnosis · Reports and development

Does a diagnostic report expire?

A report describes an assessment conducted at a particular time and for a purpose. There is no single deadline for every clinical report; administrative rules may differ and should be checked with the organisation requesting it.

By the Talk+ team 18 October 2026 3 min read
Loading reads…

Are diagnosis and report the same thing?

No. A report records the reasoning, information and conclusion of an assessment; diagnosis is a clinical conclusion under recognised criteria. An older document does not automatically cease to exist, but it may no longer describe current needs or answer a new organisation’s question.

What about school, benefits or services?

A school, insurer or service may request recent information for a specific purpose. That does not necessarily mean the clinical diagnosis has “expired”. Ask which rule applies, what information is needed, who can provide it and whether a functional summary is sufficient.

How can the child’s information be protected?

Share only necessary information, with a defined recipient and through a secure channel. Ask how it will be used and stored. A report may contain sensitive health data; do not post it in forums or send it to a service without checking that the request is legitimate.

In short

  • Clinical reports and administrative validity are not the same.
  • Check current requirements with the recipient and avoid sharing more information than needed.

Conclusion

If someone says a report has “expired”, ask for the rule and purpose behind that deadline. A recent-document requirement for a process does not erase an earlier assessment or by itself decide whether a new clinical assessment is needed.
