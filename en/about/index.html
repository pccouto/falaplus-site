About the project

About Fala+

Fala+ is an educational app for children aged 2 to 10, with word, image and sound games and communication tools.

Why was it created?

Fala+ brings together ways to communicate, explore words and play in an experience that can be part of a child’s everyday life.

Who is it for?

It is for children aged 2 to 10 and can be explored with family, at school or alongside professionals. The app is complementary and does not replace assessment or therapeutic care.

Guiding principles

  • Communication through pictures, words and voice, with ways to personalise them.
  • Activities that allow different paces and include a calm mode.
  • No advertising on the Free or Premium plans.
  • Privacy and important settings designed for adult supervision.
  • Learning through exploration, without clinical outcome promises.

Family participation

Family members can join the child, personalise words with familiar pictures and voices, and talk about what appears in games. Adult participation can enrich the experience, but it is not required to play.

Accessibility, privacy and learning

Fala+ includes words with pictures and voice, personalisation options and an adult settings area protected by a PIN. Read the privacy policy for information about data handling.
