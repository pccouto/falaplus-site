From the app to everyday play

An idea to try together

Bring a sentence from the game into a real situation with pictures or objects. Model a short sentence; the child can choose cards rather than respond with speech.

  1. 01

    After hearing or seeing a word, allow the child to respond in their preferred way. Comment on a picture without asking them to repeat.

  2. 02

    Bring a word or idea from the game into a conversation, book or everyday routine. The aim is to share, not test.

  3. 03

    An available adult can join in, but this is not required to play. Allow time to explore and stop when the child wants to.

  4. 04

    Welcome different ways to participate: watching, pointing, gestures, words or AAC. Play does not have to become a test.
