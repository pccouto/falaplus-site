Ideas for playing together

✳

From the app to everyday play

An idea to try together

Watch the pointer together before a turn and show the green area. Off screen, draw a target and point to its centre and edge without using sharp objects.

  1. 01

    Allow time to explore pointing or dragging before focusing on accuracy. Describe movements with words such as near, far, up and down.

  2. 02

    Off screen, try a target with safe materials without competing over the result.

  3. 03

    An available adult can join in, but this is not required to play. Allow time to explore and stop when the child wants to.

  4. 04

    Welcome different ways to participate: watching, pointing, gestures, words or AAC. Play does not have to become a test.
