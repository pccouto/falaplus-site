From the app to everyday play

An idea to try together

Between the bird’s flights, look at a collected picture and pause to comment. The child may prefer simply playing; conversation is not a condition for continuing.

  1. 01

    Let the child become familiar with the controls. Between attempts, pause to comment on an interesting picture.

  2. 02

    Continue off screen by choosing a picture or a word from the game, without requiring a quick response.

  3. 03

    An available adult can join in, but this is not required to play. Allow time to explore and stop when the child wants to.

  4. 04

    Welcome different ways to participate: watching, pointing, gestures, words or AAC. Play does not have to become a test.
