From the app to everyday play

An idea to try together

Display three familiar objects and cover one with a cloth. The child can point to a picture of the missing object or hide one for the adult to find.

  1. 01

    If the child wants to, look together before a turn and talk about what you noticed. Avoid turning recall into a test.

  2. 02

    Away from the screen, hide an object or swap two pictures and take turns choosing the challenge.

  3. 03

    An available adult can join in, but this is not required to play. Allow time to explore and stop when the child wants to.

  4. 04

    Welcome different ways to participate: watching, pointing, gestures, words or AAC. Play does not have to become a test.
