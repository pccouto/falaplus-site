From the app to everyday play

An idea to try together

Draw an outline on paper and fill it with large cut-out shapes. Which piece fits the tip? Show one possibility and let the child try another.

  1. 01

    Before moving a piece, invite the child to show where they want it to go. They can try an idea and try again.

  2. 02

    After playing, explore shapes, pairs or paths using real objects. Let the child choose how to continue.

  3. 03

    An available adult can join in, but this is not required to play. Allow time to explore and stop when the child wants to.

  4. 04

    Welcome different ways to participate: watching, pointing, gestures, words or AAC. Play does not have to become a test.
