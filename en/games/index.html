At each child’s pace

Play is a way to connect.

Games can create moments to share. When there are questions about speech or development, professional support remains essential.

01

Speech therapy and speech delay

If you are looking for games to complement speech therapy or support communication when there is a speech delay, choose activities around the child’s interests and discuss relevant goals with their professional. Playing a game is not speech therapy.

Read the article
02

Childhood apraxia of speech

This game is not a treatment for childhood apraxia of speech. It should not be presented as a speech motor planning exercise or replace an individual plan set by a speech-language pathologist.

Read the article
03

Autism and augmentative and alternative communication (AAC)

For an autistic child, adapt the activity to their preferences, sensory comfort and ways of communicating. Accept gestures, pointing or AAC without requiring speech to participate. Choose a game around the child, rather than a diagnosis alone.

Read the article
This is an educational game, not an assessment. Scores cannot establish conclusions about a child’s development. Talk+ does not replace professional assessment or support.
