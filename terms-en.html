01 Who we are

Fala+ is developed and provided by Paulo César dos Santos Couto, based in Portugal, identified as a trader on Google Play. Contact: apoio@falamais.pt.

02 What Fala+ is

Fala+ is an educational app for children, with games, activities and a communication board (Communicate), designed to be used with an adult's support. The app is distributed through Google Play.

03 Free plan and Fala+ Premium

Fala+ can be used for free, with access to part of the content (for example, the first levels of the Walk and one child profile). The optional Fala+ Premium subscription unlocks additional content and features. What each plan includes is shown in the app, under Adult Area → Plan, before any purchase.

04 Subscriptions, renewal and cancellation

Fala+ Premium is available as monthly and yearly plans, purchased and managed exclusively through Google Play. The price, in your currency, is always shown by Google Play before you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period, at the price then in force, until cancelled. You can cancel at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions; Premium access remains until the end of the period already paid. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.
